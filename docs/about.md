# How these notes work

These are a learner's working notes, published as they are written. They are not a textbook and not a solutions manual. They are a record of the moment each idea clicked.

## The note template

Every chapter note has the same six parts:

1. **Big idea.** The chapter in a few sentences.
2. **Key results.** The definitions, formulas and theorems worth remembering.
3. **My derivation.** At least one result worked out by hand, not copied.
4. **Where it shows up.** Connections to other fields, systems, and everyday life.
5. **Exercises I solved.** Which ones, and the trick that cracked each.
6. **Still fuzzy.** Honest open questions. These shrink as the note matures.

## Built to be light

This site is meant to load on a slow phone connection anywhere in the world. It uses your device's own fonts, renders math with a small self-hosted library only on pages that need it, and has no ads, trackers, or analytics.

Solutions here are my own attempts, shared to show the thinking. If you are taking a course that uses CLRS, please work the problems yourself first.
